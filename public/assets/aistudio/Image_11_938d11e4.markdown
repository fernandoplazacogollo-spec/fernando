## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an experimental operations command center interface designed for technical operators, telemetry supervisors, and mission-critical workflows. The aesthetic couples high-density utility with cinematic precision, rejecting conventional corporate flatness in favor of an engineered console experience.

Visual principles:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep slate canvas tiers create a low-luminance substrate where active telemetry elements stand out with distinct focal priority.
- **Instrument Precision:** Geometry and layouts prioritize structural alignment, hairline visual delimiters, and condensed technical typography reminiscent of heads-up telemetry monitors.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Color is deployed strictly as signal data. Active states, interactive affordances, and alert vectors cut through dark structural backdrops with concentrated indigo and cyan luminescence.

## Colors

The palette operates on a high-contrast dark substrate calibrated for prolonged situational awareness in low-light environments.

- **Primary (`#6366F1`):** Electric Indigo acts as the primary interactive driver and focal accent. It commands key action pathways, focused input states, and active data clusters.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4`):** Cyber Cyan represents auxiliary telemetry, secondary interactions, system statuses, and network linkages.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** Biosphere Emerald handles nominal system validation, live feed indicators, and success operations.
- **Neutral (`#0B0F19`):** Deep Obsidian Slate forms the root canvas. Layered surface tiers scale through `#111827` (Container Base), `#1F2937` (Container Elevated), up to `#374151` (Structural Outlines).
- **Functional Semantics:** Critical errors leverage sharp Coral (`#EF4444`); advisory conditions utilize Signal Amber (`#F59E0B`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes technical geometric display titles in **Space Grotesk** against the neutral, ultra-legible monospace-leaning cadence of **Geist** for dense operational data.

- **Display & Headlines:** Space Grotesk injects structural forward-momentum into dashboard metrics, station designations, and operational readouts. Large figures should maintain tight negative letter-spacing for a chiseled mechanical posture.
- **Body & Numerical Readouts:** Geist handles continuous logs, metadata, inputs, and tabular reports. Its precise glyph geometry prevents character ambiguity in dense alphanumeric tables.
- **Labels & Micro-Indicators:** Rendered in uppercase with extended letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to maintain legibility when rendered against low-contrast surfaces at micro dimensions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout enforces an 8-point baseline mathematical rhythm optimized for high-density command dashboards and modular operations telemetry.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column dynamic fluid grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` screen edge margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Secondary console modules collapse into tabbed sidebars.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column single-stack rail with `1rem` margins and condensed `0.5rem` component internal gaps to conserve viewport height.
- **Density Guidelines:** Component internal spacing adheres to strict technical tolerances: standard operational cards employ `space-lg` outer padding and `space-sm` internal metadata gaps, allowing maximum simultaneous signal visibility without cognitive occlusion.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, decorative drop shadows. Visual depth is established through stacked obsidian structural planes, translucent hairline strokes, and high-frequency edge highlights.

- **Tiers of Presence:**
  - **Layer 0 (Canvas Substrate):** `#0B0F19` base canvas.
  - **Layer 1 (Card & Module Deck):** `#111827` surface accented with a single-pixel top highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) and border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
  - **Layer 2 (Overlays & Flyouts):** `#1F2937` surface paired with an outer edge stroke of `#374151` and a low-bleed perimeter shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`).
  - **Active Luminescence (HUD State):** Interactive elements or critical monitors use a concentrated colored glow (`0 0 16px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)`) to signal focus or live broadcast modes.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a precise, angular shape vocabulary (Level `1` - Soft). 

- **Base Radius (4px / 0.25rem):** Applied to buttons, input fields, tags, indicators, and inline elements. This tight curvature preserves the feeling of machined hardware chassis and technical equipment.
- **Card & Panel Radius (8px / 0.5rem):** Applied to major telemetry cards, data grids, and floating operational panels (`rounded-lg`).
- **Modal & Structural HUD Radius (12px / 0.75rem):** Applied exclusively to top-level viewports and dialog prompts (`rounded-xl`).
- **Pill Exceptions:** Strictly reserved for round active status pips and circular counter badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#6366F1` background, white label text (`label-md`), 4px border radius. Hover introduces a subtle cyan shift with a luminous drop shadow (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary:** Dark surface `#1F2937` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text in `#E5E7EB`. On hover, border color transitions to `#6366F1`.
- **Destructive:** Dark crimson base (`#1A0B0E`) with an outer border of `#EF4444` and text in `#FCA5A5`.

### Chips & Telemetry Tags
- Compact height (22px), uppercase typography (`label-sm`), 4px radius. 
- Translucent backgrounds: Nominal (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)` with `#10B981` text), Warning (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)` with `#F59E0B` text), Alert (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)` with `#EF4444` text).

### Form Inputs & Terminal Controls
- Background in `#0F172A`, enclosed by a 1px border of `#374151`.
- Typography set in `Geist` (`body-md`) with high-contrast text (`#F9FAFB`) and subdued placeholders (`#6B7280`).
- Active focus state overrides border with a crisp `#6366F1` ring and a 2px inner glow.

### Checkboxes & Switch Controls
- Square 16px checkboxes with a 2px corner radius. Inactive borders sit at `#4B5563`. Checked state applies `#6366F1` fill with an offset terminal checkmark icon.
- Hardware-style toggles: 36px track width, sliding square indicator block with micro-chamfered edges.

### Cards & Module Enclosures
- Background `#111827`, bordered by 1px stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- Card headers incorporate an integrated technical breadcrumb or monospace coordinates tag in `#9CA3AF`.

### Specialized Command Components
- **Live Signal Tracker:** Real-time pulse ping comprising an animated emerald dot (`#10B981`) enveloped in expanding radar pulse rings.
- **Metric Stream Units:** Key stat containers featuring high-scale numerical values rendered in `Space Grotesk` with inline delta sparklines and unit suffix micro-labels.
